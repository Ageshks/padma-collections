1. Who we are, and what this covers

Padma Collections ("we", "us") operates the Padma Collections Android app ("the App"), an online shop for imitation jewellery.

This policy explains what personal information the App collects, why it collects it, and what you can do about it. It applies to the App on Google Play.

We do not collect or store any payment card details. The App has no online payment facility — see section 4.

3. Why we use your information

  • To create and maintain your account, and to let you sign in.
  • To take, process and deliver your order, including contacting you about it.
  • To support you, including over WhatsApp if you choose to contact us there.
  • To send you order updates and offers, where you have agreed to receive them.
  • To show you relevant products, and to fix faults so the App keeps working.
  • To detect and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To meet our legal and tax obligations.

We rely on your consent for optional uses (such as marketing messages), and on performing the contract you enter into — delivering the order you asked for — for the steps needed to fulfil it.

5. What we do not collect

The App does not collect or request:
  • Your precise or approximate location — we request no location permission.
  • Your contacts, call log, or SMS messages.
  • Your camera, microphone, photos or gallery.
  • Any advertising identifier. The App contains no advertising SDK and builds no advertising profile.
  • Your biometric, health, religious or political data.

The only permissions the App requests are internet access, network status, and — on Android 13 and later — permission to show notifications. You may decline notifications; browsing and shopping still work.

6. Cookies and advertising

The App uses no cookies or web tracking, because it is a native Android app rather than a website. It carries no advertising, and does not use Google's Advertising ID or share personal information with advertising networks.

This policy page itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts.

8. How we protect your information

We use industry-standard measures appropriate to a small online shop:

  • All data travels over encrypted HTTPS connections in both directions.
  • Your account is protected by a password. Signing in with Google uses Google's secure sign-in — we never see your Google password.
  • Access to customer data is limited to staff who need it to fulfil orders.
  • Firebase security rules restrict who may read or write customer records, so a customer cannot see another customer's orders.
  • Only administrators may change store content such as products and images.
No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you are under 18, please do not create an account or place an order without a parent or guardian's involvement.

If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us at the address in section 13 and we will delete it.

10. Your rights and controls

Subject to Indian law — including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — you have the right to:

  • Access — ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct — ask us to fix anything inaccurate. You can edit your name, phone number and profile in the App at any time.
  • Delete — ask us to erase your information (see section 11).
  • Withdraw consent — stop marketing messages at any time through your notification settings or by replying “STOP” to a marketing message.
  • Nominate someone — exercise these rights on your behalf, where the law permits it.
  • Complain — raise a grievance with us, and if unresolved with us, with the Data Protection Board of India.

We respond to verified requests within the timeframes the law requires. We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on a request, so that we do not disclose your information to someone else.

11. Deleting your account and your data

You can request deletion of your account and the personal data associated with it.

How to request deletion

Email us from the same address registered to your account, with the subject line "Delete my account", and include the email address you sign in with. We will verify that you own the account, then delete it.

What we delete

  • Your account record, cart and wishlist.
  • Your profile details, such as name, email address and phone number.

What we may need to keep

Some records cannot be deleted immediately, because we are legally required to retain them:

  • Order and invoice records. We are required to keep sales and tax records for accounting and tax purposes. After the statutory retention period expires, we delete or anonymise them.
  • Records needed to establish or defend legal claims.
Retained order records are kept in a restricted form and are no longer used to market to you or to operate your account. Where we hold data solely on your behalf, we delete it; where we hold it because the law requires it, we explain why.

What happens to your order history

Once your account is deleted you will no longer be able to sign in or view past orders in the App. If you need a copy of an invoice before deleting, please ask us first.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time — for example, if we add a feature that changes what we collect, or if the law changes.

When we make a material change, we will update the “last updated” date at the bottom of this page and, where the change is significant, notify you in the App or by email.
