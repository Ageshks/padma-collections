Request deletion

You can ask us at any time to delete your Padma Collections account and the personal data associated with it.

How to request it

  1. Email us from the same email address you sign in to the app with.
  2. Use the subject line “Delete my account”.
  3. Send the message to hello@padmacollections.com.
Example:
Subject: Delete my account
Hello, please delete my Padma Collections account and its associated data. My account email is you@example.com.

What happens next

  • We will verify that you own the account, so that we do not delete it by mistake.
  • We will then delete your account, your profile details, your cart and your wishlist.
  • You will be able to place a new order without an account, but you will lose access to your order history in the app.

What we have to keep

Some records cannot be deleted straight away, because the law requires us to retain them:

  • Order and invoice records. We must keep sales and tax records for accounting and tax purposes. These are deleted or anonymised once the statutory retention period ends.
  • Records needed to establish or defend a legal claim.

Retained order records are no longer used to market to you or to operate an account.

Please note: before deleting your account, save any invoice you need — you will not be able to view past orders in the app afterwards.
